# User Needs

## 1. Primary Needs

- Persistent organization and retrieval of personal knowledge across multiple domains
- Reliable system that doesn't automatically treat AI-generated information as canonical truth
- Ability to understand the provenance, reasoning, and evolution of important information
- Clear boundaries between different types of knowledge (personal, fictional, learning, research)
- Control over what becomes part of their persistent knowledge base

## 2. Secondary Needs

- Automated analysis and relationship identification between pieces of knowledge
- Conflict detection and contradiction resolution capabilities
- Historical versioning and change tracking for important knowledge
- Efficient review workflows for proposed knowledge changes
- Integration of multiple input sources (text, documents, audio, video, etc.)

## 3. Long-Term Needs

- Progressive autonomy in AI assistance while maintaining human control over canonical knowledge
- Advanced reasoning capabilities across interconnected knowledge
- Ability to identify and manage stale or outdated derived knowledge
- Comprehensive dashboard for knowledge health monitoring
- Seamless integration of specialized modules (learning, research, planning) with shared knowledge foundation

## 4. Pain Points

- Information fragmentation across different tools and platforms
- Difficulty in organizing and retrieving personal knowledge over time
- Uncertainty about the reliability and source of information in their knowledge base
- Manual effort required to maintain relationships between different pieces of knowledge
- Lack of clear separation between different types of knowledge (personal facts vs. fictional content)
- Over-reliance on tools that automatically treat AI inference as fact

## 5. Current Workarounds

- Using multiple separate applications for different knowledge domains
- Manual organization and cross-referencing of information
- Maintaining physical or digital notebooks for important personal facts
- Creating spreadsheets or databases to track relationships between information
- Relying on memory or external tools for contradiction detection

## 6. Desired Outcomes

- A single, unified system that organizes all personal knowledge reliably
- AI assistance that automates the work of organizing, extracting, connecting, and analyzing information while keeping the user in control
- Clear visibility into how important information was derived and validated
- Automatic conflict detection and resolution workflows
- Ability to understand potential consequences of changes to important knowledge

## 7. Open Questions

- What specific initial V1 capabilities should be prioritized?
- How should the review queue experience be designed for efficiency and usability?
- What level of automation is appropriate for different types of knowledge validation?
- How should domain separation be implemented conceptually without technical implementation details?
- What are the most critical user workflows that need to be supported in the initial release?
