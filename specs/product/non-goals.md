# Product Non-Goals

This document records things that Pekopeko explicitly does NOT aim to become.

## Current Non-Goals

- Building a generic consumer AI assistant for a mass market
- Maximizing autonomous behavior at the expense of reliability
- Automatically treating every AI inference as canonical truth
- Automatically merging fictional knowledge with real-world personal knowledge
- Replacing the user's judgment about important personal facts or decisions
- Choosing implementation technologies during the product-definition phase
- Designing the final architecture before the product and domain models are understood

## Future Non-Goals

- Unrestricted autonomous execution of canonical knowledge changes
- Automatic ingestion of external sources as personal canonical knowledge
- Elimination of human validation for important knowledge decisions
- Complete automation of all knowledge management workflows
- Replacing human judgment in critical decision-making processes

## Explicitly Rejected Directions

- Building a simple chatbot or isolated second-brain application (the vision is toward a more sophisticated agentic system)
- Implementing any specific database, vector store, or other technology choices at this product definition stage
- Designing final agent architecture or implementation details during the product-definition phase
- Creating V1 requirements from long-term conceptual ideas without sufficient validation

Do not invent arbitrary restrictions.
